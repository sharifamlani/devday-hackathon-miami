# Demo data

A fixture set for rehearsing file upload, donor normalization, duplicate suggestions, and gift-history analysis.

## Files to upload

1. `team-call-list.csv` — team contacts; Christian Gonzalez is first and has the user-provided demo phone. The other teammates have explicitly fictional 555 numbers.
2. `tech-philanthropist-gifts.csv` — structured gift-event rows for 80 technology founders, executives, investors, and philanthropists.
3. `tech-philanthropist-profiles.txt` — plain-text profiles and gift histories for 20 additional people. Together, the two prospect files cover 100 distinct profiles.

Upload the two prospect files to the Givers tab. Upload the team list separately to Neighbors or the app's contact/non-donor option. Confirm consent before placing any demo call.

## Data provenance and safe use

- Teammate names and Christian's phone number came from the user. The other team phone numbers use fictional 305-555-01xx placeholders and must not be called.
- Prospect names and public giving channels are research leads, not evidence of a relationship with the hackathon, its partners, or the fictional South Florida Community Demo Fund.
- All prospect email addresses use `example.org`; phone numbers are synthetic 305-555 placeholders.
- Every prospect gift, amount, date, and gift channel in the upload files is invented demo data. It is not a claim that a named person made a donation to any actual nonprofit.
- The research list includes technology-connected people and a few ecosystem-adjacent philanthropists. Interest areas are high-level summaries, not eligibility or likelihood scores. Verify each organization's current priorities and application process before real outreach.
- Forbes's ranking is called the **Forbes 400**, not “Forbes 500.” This is a selected demo roster, not a reproduction of a Forbes ranking.

Do not upload this README or `SOURCES.md`; they are documentation, not fixture data.
