# Research notes and sources

Checked November 8, 2026. This is lightweight prospect research for a hackathon fixture, not due diligence. It records public organizations or broad public-interest themes associated with the people in the sample. Inclusion does not establish personal grant authority, current giving, local eligibility, a relationship with the demo nonprofit, or likelihood to donate.

## Highest-priority examples

- **Jeff Bezos / Bezos Day 1 Families Fund:** the Fund's 2025 awardee list includes Care Resource Community Health Centers in Miami ($5 million), supporting family homelessness and stability work. This is a concrete South Florida connection, but awards are selected by the Fund and do not imply open individual outreach. [Fund and awardees](https://www.bezosdayonefund.org/day1familiesfund)
- **Michael and Susan Dell / Michael & Susan Dell Foundation:** public U.S. focus includes education, health, and family economic stability. Review current program criteria and geography before considering fit. [U.S. work](https://www.dell.org/united-states/) · [Partnerships](https://www.dell.org/partnerships/)
- **Bill Gates / Gates Foundation:** 2026 annual letter sets out foundation priorities through 2045. The Foundation says most grants go to organizations identified by staff, with specific RFPs also available. [2026 letter](https://www.gatesfoundation.org/ideas/articles/2026-gates-foundation-annual-letter/) · [Grant opportunities](https://www.gatesfoundation.org/about/how-we-work/grant-opportunities)
- **Sergey Brin / Sergey Brin Family Foundation:** public IRS filing records and filing-based grant summaries show sizable foundation grantmaking, including science and climate recipients. These are historical foundation grants, not personal gifts to the demo organization. [ProPublica filing](https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/organizations/472107200/202533169349103858/full) · [Filing-based summary](https://data.useplinth.com/foundation/sergey-brin-family-foundation-472107200)
- **Mark Zuckerberg and Priscilla Chan / Chan Zuckerberg Initiative and Biohub:** Biohub describes biomedical research and science tools, including an AI-and-biology initiative. This is a focused research channel, not a general community grant program. [Biohub initiative](https://biohub.org/news/ai-biology-cure-disease/)

## Fixture boundary

The project-facing files contain fictional gift histories and synthetic contact details. They are designed to exercise one-row-per-gift ingestion, donor grouping, recency calculations, and text extraction. Never use them for real prospect outreach or donor scoring. Replace synthetic facts with permissioned, verified records before any real deployment.
