### What the whole panel rewards (synthesis)

1. **It runs live, instantly, with no setup.** Lerman scrapped his own clunky demo for an instant tour. Leon
   wants verifiable output. Kurz's event says "not slide decks, shipped software."
2. **A non-technical nonprofit staffer can use it Monday.** That is McMillan's whole framing, and the two
   Foundation judges see small nonprofits with no staff every day.
3. **It is a real agent, not a chat wrapper.** Sharma's talk is literally "Beyond Chatbots". He and Leon
   will look for the workflow: steps, tools, data and guardrails. Show the tool calls.
4. **Every claim traces to a source.** Mohan (audits grants), Kessell (data) and Leon ("machine-checkable
   evidence") all point the same way. This matches our lessons-learned rule 5.
5. **It is local.** Stern, Kurz and the Foundation all lead with Miami. Name the nonprofit, the
   neighborhood and the date (Give Miami Day, Thu Nov 19).
6. **Short and memorable, explained plainly.** Lerman values story. Leon: "explaining a system out loud
   is the only reliable way to find out whether you actually understand it."

**How our shortlist shapes fit:** **A (Give Miami Day kit)** hits the Foundation, the local angle and
"Monday". **B (Grant Report Autopilot)** with click-to-source numbers is aimed squarely at Mohan, Kessell
and Leon. Either way, put the agent's tool calls on screen for Sharma and keep the demo to zero setup for
Lerman.

## The Miami Foundation (title sponsor, two judges)

- **Five priorities** ✅ (team page): Empowering Youth & Families · Eliminating Inequities · Generating
  Prosperity · Building Climate & Disaster Resilience · Inspiring Civic Involvement. Houses nearly 1,000
  community efforts.
- **Community Grants 2025–27** 🔸: two-year **unrestricted general operating support**, about $6–7M across
  seven funding priorities (sources conflict on the total).
- **Give Miami Day** 🔸: the 2025 edition raised a record **~$43M for 1,400+ nonprofits from 60,000+ donors**
  (WLRN), up from $39.5M in 2024. Education is the largest area funded. **2026: Thu Nov 19**, early giving
  Nov 14–18.
- **Data initiative** 🔸: they are building a shareable community data resource from Give Miami Day
  registrations and grant data. VP Community Investments Lindsey Linzer, quoted in a search summary (verify
  before quoting), called Give Miami Day data "the most complete, richest source of data" and said it showed
  **~30% of local nonprofits have almost no budget flexibility**.

## Howard Lerman: Roam

- **Role** ✅: founder and CEO of Roam (ro.am), a virtual office / "cloud HQ" (launched 2022, $40M raised).
  He co-founded Yext and was its CEO through the IPO.
- **Now** ✅: pitching an "Office of the Future" in which **AI agents work alongside people**. He calls today's
  agent orchestration "siloed, not collaborative and async" and wants agents working in the open:
  teammates joining an agent session, agents summoned into meetings. He replaced Roam's old demo ("too
  clunky", up to 10 minutes with permission prompts) with an instant interactive tour. The new site was
  built with Claude and Codex.
- **Other views** 🔸 (secondhand summary): show the product live despite the glitch risk; ship constantly
  (56 releases in 56 weeks); make the story memorable; value proven performance over prestige.
- **Likely to impress:** an instant, zero-friction live demo; agents whose work is visible and shared;
  something used for real on Monday; a short story people repeat. **Turn off:** setup screens,
  permission walls, slides instead of product, a long pitch.
- Sources: [The Office That Thinks](https://ro.am/ideas/the-office-that-thinks) ·
  [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/11/02/former-yext-ceo-launches-roam-to-provide-a-virtual-hq-for-distributed-teams) ·
  [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2023/08/04/roam-founder-howard-lerman-interview-cloud-hq-remote-hybrid-work) ·
